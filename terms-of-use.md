# Terms of Use for Tiansui Almanac

Last updated: April 25, 2026

## 1. Service description

Tiansui Almanac provides traditional almanac references, Tiansui study notes, Five Elements context, and saved observations for cultural study and self-reflection.

## 2. Intended use

The app is intended for self-discovery, education, and entertainment. It does not provide medical, legal, financial, or other regulated professional advice.

## 3. User input

You may choose whether to enter name, birth date, birth time, and timezone. These inputs are used to generate your study-reference experience in the current version.

## 4. Product changes

Tiansui Almanac may evolve over time. If future versions introduce subscriptions, cloud sync, analytics, or account systems, the related policies and disclosures will be updated accordingly.
