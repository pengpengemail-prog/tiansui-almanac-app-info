# TAME Tiansui: Solar Planner · Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-11-10. This policy applies to the redesigned iOS planner introduced in build 50. Earlier versions are described in the historical policy below.

The app stores your study plans on your device. A plan can include a title, date, completion status, reading source, and notes. The app does not require an account. The developer does not receive your plans or track you across apps.

If you confirm adding an event to Apple Calendar, the app asks for permission to add events and writes the title, start time, and end time from the preview. It does not write your reading source or notes to Calendar. Your calendar account may sync the event. Manage permission in iOS Settings and manage events in Calendar.

When an export is available and you choose to share a plan, the system share sheet passes the export to the destination you choose. If you restore a previous purchase, Apple handles the request and the app reads StoreKit entitlement information to determine whether export is available. If you open a cited source link, the destination site applies its own privacy policy.

Plans remain on your device while its app data is retained. Removing that data does not remove events or files already sent elsewhere. Use the support page for privacy questions.

## 隐私政策

研读计划保存在设备上，可以包含标题、日期、完成状态、研读出处和笔记。App 无需注册；开发者不接收这些计划，也不进行跨 App 跟踪。

在预览页确认添加到 Apple 日历后，App 才请求添加事件的权限，并写入预览中的标题、开始时间和结束时间。研读出处和笔记不会写入日历。日历账户可能同步事件。你可在 iOS 设置中管理权限，在日历中管理事件。

如可使用导出功能，你主动分享计划时，系统分享菜单会把导出内容交给你选择的目标。恢复历史购买时，由 Apple 处理请求，App 根据 StoreKit 权益判断导出功能是否可用。打开研读出处的外部链接时，目标网站适用其自己的隐私政策。

计划随设备上的 App 数据保留。移除 App 数据不会清除已加入日历的事件或已分享的文件。如有隐私问题，请通过支持页联系。

# Privacy Policy for Tiansui Almanac

Effective date: April 16, 2026

## 1. Overview

Tiansui Almanac respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the current version of the Tiansui Almanac iOS app.

Tiansui Almanac is a traditional almanac, date-reference, and Tiansui study app. In the current version, users can enter birth-related information to generate traditional study references directly in the app.

The current version is designed to process study references locally on device and does not require account creation.

## 2. Information you enter

To generate a study reference, you may enter:

- name, optionally
- birth date
- birth time
- timezone

In the current version, this information is used to generate your study reference inside the app.

## 3. Data collection

In the current version, Tiansui Almanac does not collect personal data from you on behalf of the developer.

Specifically, the current version does not include:

- account registration
- user login
- advertising SDKs
- third-party analytics SDKs
- cloud profile storage
- social posting features

## 4. How information is processed

In the current version, study-reference generation is intended to happen locally on your device. Information you enter is used to calculate and display traditional reference notes within the app experience.

## 5. Tracking

Tiansui Almanac does not track you across apps or websites in the current version.

## 6. Data sharing

In the current version, Tiansui Almanac does not sell your personal information and does not share your entered study information with third parties for advertising purposes.

## 7. Data retention

In the current version, no developer-hosted user account database is used for storing your study profile. If future versions introduce account features, sync, subscriptions, analytics, or customer support tooling, this Privacy Policy will be updated before those features are released.

## 8. Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect product changes, legal requirements, or operational updates. When material changes are made, the updated policy will be posted with a new effective date.
